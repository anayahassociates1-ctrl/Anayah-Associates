FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS • SHRIRAMPUR & AHILYANAGAR

Answers to common questions about house plans, sanctions & construction

Everything you need to know about working with an architect, municipal approvals, 3D designs, and construction quality standards.

Who is the best architect and civil engineer in Shrirampur?

Anayah Associates, led by Er. Sameer Subhedar Shaikh since 2018, is widely recognized as the premier civil engineering and architectural consultancy in Shrirampur and Ahilyanagar district. With over 60+ completed residential, commercial, and industrial landmarks, we deliver custom Vastu house plans, photorealistic 3D elevations, MahaBPMS municipal sanction, and earthquake-resistant structural design under one roof.

How can I get building plan approval (MahaBPMS Sanction) in Shrirampur and Ahilyanagar?

Anayah Associates prepares complete AutoDCR drawings, ownership document verification (7/12 extract, city survey PR card), zonal compliance, and submits direct filings through Maharashtra's MahaBPMS portal for fast, hassle-free municipal sanction. We coordinate directly with local municipal councils and town planning authorities so your project is officially approved without unnecessary delays.

What services does Anayah Associates provide?

We provide comprehensive architectural and civil engineering services:

  • Architectural House Plans: Custom Vastu-compliant 2D plans, layouts, and working drawings.
  • 3D Exterior & Interior Elevations: High-definition photorealistic 3D renders with day & night views.
  • MahaBPMS Sanctions: Building permission and AutoDCR approvals for town planning.
  • RC Structural Design: Earthquake-resistant concrete design compliant with IS 456:2000 and IS 1893:2016.
  • Turnkey Construction: Complete material contracting, structural execution, and quality-controlled handover.
How much time does it take for 3D elevations and structural designs?

Concept 2D floor plans and 3D exterior elevations are typically prepared within 3 to 7 working days depending on project complexity. Complete structural analysis and reinforcement detailing drawings (foundation, columns, beams, slabs) are delivered within 5 to 10 days, ensuring your on-site contractor has accurate blueprints to begin work immediately.

Does Anayah Associates provide on-site supervision during construction?

Yes, absolutely. Every project undergoes direct structural supervision by qualified civil engineers at every critical stage: footing excavation, column marking, plinth beam casting, slab reinforcement verification, and concrete pouring quality checks. We never outsource critical structural execution to unchecked contractors.

Which areas do you serve outside Shrirampur?

While our head office is located at Sai Super Market, Main Road, Shrirampur, we actively serve clients across Ahilyanagar district and western Maharashtra, including Rahuri, Deolali Pravara, Newasa, Kopargaon, Sangamner, Babhaleshwar, Loni, and Pune.
